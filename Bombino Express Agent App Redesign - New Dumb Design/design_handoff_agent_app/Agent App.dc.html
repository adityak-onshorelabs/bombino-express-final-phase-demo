Bombino Express · Agent surface · Redesign

One document per screen, not a pile of boxes

Same palette as the customer app — Admiralty navy, Dispatch Amber, Poppins — with a harder grammar of its own, taken from the freight documents this business runs on. Cards inside cards are gone: each screen is one ruled sheet. Emphasis comes from type weight and from a single hairline, never from another border.

Colour law

Amber is money. Navy is a state change. Red is late. Nothing else is coloured.

Type

Four sizes only: 20 name, 14 address, 12 body, 10 mono label. No 11/13/15 drift.

Structure

One sheet, full-bleed rows, 4px corners, hairline dividers. Never a border inside a border.

Taps

The next action sits on the row it belongs to, or in a fixed bar at thumb height. 56px minimum.
