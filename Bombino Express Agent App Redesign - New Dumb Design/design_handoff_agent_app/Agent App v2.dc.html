Bombino Express · Agent app · v2 — simple

Same look, far less on it

Nothing about the visual system changed. Each screen lost about a third of what it held, the order number became the loudest thing on every card, and every label is now one or two short words. An agent should be able to read a screen at arm's length and know what to tap.

Order number first

Every card opens with a navy strip carrying the number at 20px mono, white on navy. It is what the agent says on the phone and what ops asks for.

Short words

Take job. Picked up. Take money. Call. Map. Off. No sentence on a button, no paragraph on a screen.

One thing per screen

Counts, tips, notes and second amounts are gone. What is left is the job in hand and the next one.
